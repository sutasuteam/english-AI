Daily Lesson

📖 Story

Tom loves learning English. Every morning he studies new words and practices speaking with his friends. One day he spoke confidently with a tourist and felt proud of his progress.

📚 Vocabulary

Confidently = Dengan percaya diri
Progress = Kemajuan
Practice = Berlatih

✍ Grammar Focus

Simple Present Tense

Tom loves learning English.

Digunakan untuk aktivitas rutin atau kebiasaan sehari-hari.

❓ Questions

1. What does Tom study every morning?
2. Who did Tom speak with?
3. How did Tom feel?

Write 3 sentences about your English learning journey.
